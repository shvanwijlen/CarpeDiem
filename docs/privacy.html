Carpe Diem Boats - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Carpe Diem Boats ("the app") is a companion app that shows live data from a boat that runs the CarpeDiem system: a Raspberry Pi on the boat that sends its data to a server you run yourself. This policy explains what the app does with data.

The short version

The developer collects no data about you. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. Everything the app shows comes from a server that you set up and control, and it goes nowhere else.

What the app stores on your device

  • Connection settings you enter: the address of your data store, and optionally the address of the Raspberry Pi on your boat, plus app preferences. These stay on your device.
  • Access keys you enter (the read key for your data store and, optionally, the Pi's key). These are kept in the iOS Keychain, on this device only. They are not included in backups or restored onto other devices.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

What the app connects to

The app connects only to the addresses you enter: your own data store (over HTTPS) and, if you set it, your own Raspberry Pi on the boat's network for live camera views. It sends your access key to that server so that it can authorize the request. The data the app displays (for example the boat's position, battery, weather and camera images) is loaded from your server and shown on screen. The developer never receives it.

In demo mode the app shows built-in sample data and makes no network connections.

Face ID and passcode

If you turn on "Lock with Face ID", the app asks iOS to verify you. iOS handles the check itself; the app only learns whether it succeeded. It never receives, stores or transmits biometric data.

Permissions

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. It asks for local network access only so it can reach your Raspberry Pi when you have entered its address.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
